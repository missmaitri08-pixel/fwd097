Guest House Services & Amenities

Comprehensive hospitality and academic support services designed for a comfortable stay at South Asian University.

Hospitality & Campus Services

Tailored amenities ensuring convenience, comfort, and productivity for university visitors.

📶

WI-FI

Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi access available 24/7 across guest rooms and common areas for secure browsing, streaming, and communication.

🅿️

On-Site Parking

Secure, well-maintained, and easily accessible on-site parking within the guest house premises, free of charge on a first-come, first-served basis for registered guests.

🏊

Swimming Pool

Well-maintained swimming pool available exclusively for guests during designated daily hours, complete with poolside seating and towels.

🏋️

Gymnasium

Modern fitness facility open daily for guests with treadmills, exercise bikes, free weights, and strength-training machines.

🧘

Yoga & Meditation Center

Calm and serene space for self-guided or group morning yoga and meditation sessions, with mats and basic props provided on-site.

🍽️

Cafeteria

Freshly prepared daily meals, small gatherings, and event catering with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options, plus customized menus on request.

🧺

Laundry (On request and chargeble)

Washing, drying, and ironing services available at nominal charges on request, with same-day service for morning requests.

🛗

Lift

Spacious, regularly maintained modern elevators across all floors for convenient and accessible vertical movement, including for guests with mobility needs.

📹

CCTV

24/7 CCTV surveillance covering entrances, corridors, and shared common areas for guest safety while respecting privacy in private rooms.

🛎️

Front Desk

Staffed round-the-clock (24/7) to assist with late check-ins, local information, and on-stay support at any hour.

🧹

House Keeping Services

Round-the-clock room cleaning and upkeep to keep rooms hygienic and comfortable whenever needed.

🚿

Hot water

Continuous hot water supply across all bathrooms for a warm shower or bath anytime during your stay.

🛁

Attached bathrooms

Clean, well-maintained attached bathrooms in every guest room with modern fittings and essential toiletries.

❄️

Air-conditioned

Modern air conditioning in guest rooms to keep your stay cool and comfortable in any weather.

🚨

Fire Safety with Smoke Alarm System

Smoke alarms and fire safety systems installed across rooms and common areas, regularly tested and maintained for early detection and prompt response.

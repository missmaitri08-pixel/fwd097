Our Accommodation Categories

The accommodation charges (listed below) do not include breakfast, lunch, or dinner, which may be availed separately on a chargeable basis at the cafeteria.

  • Standard Room (₹1,000 – ₹2,000 / day): Single & Double occupancy rates vary by category (University Guests, Staff Family, Parents, External Students, Private Guests, Drivers).
  • VIP Suite (₹2,000 – ₹4,000 / day): Premium accommodation available for University Guests/Invitees and Faculty/Staff Guests & Family Members.
  • Halls & Meeting Rooms (₹500 – ₹1,000 / day): Exclusive facility reservations for SAU Faculty and Staff (Banquet Hall, Party Hall, and Meeting Rooms).

Additional Guidelines & Special Terms

  1. SAU officials availing the facility due to official work will be allowed with no charges on the recommendation of Head of the Department.
  2. Newly recruited staff can stay in the Guest House:
    • For first ten days @ ₹500/- per day
    • Thereafter, @ ₹1,000/- per day or HRA whichever is lower for a maximum period of two weeks after first ten days, if allotment of house is pending.
  3. Get-together of University faculty members/staff can be hosted with one-month prior notice and signed from the concerned head of the Department. For such functions/parties (where payment is made out of official funds or contributed collectively/personally), charges must be paid 7 days in advance.

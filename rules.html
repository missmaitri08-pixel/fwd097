1. BookingHow to request a room and who gets priority
  1. Booking requests for rooms for conferences, workshops, individual academic visitors, or any other official purposes shall be made at least 15 days in advance through the SAU website. Room bookings may also be made online through: https://guesthouse.sau.int
  2. Contact Details of the Guest House:
    Email: guesthouse@sau.int (Copy to: gro@sau.int)
    Phone no: Reception – 011-35656823 Ext. No. 6823
  3. Guest House Requisition Form is attached. It is compulsory to fill all the information in the form.
  4. Extension of booking will not be automatic. However, it may be considered subject to availability of accommodation and on the request of the initiating Department/Institution/Staff/Person.
  5. Students will not be accommodated in the Guest House.
  6. All official bookings should be duly endorsed by the Head of the Department/Dean of the Faculty/Head of the Administrative Unit/Professor of the Department.
  7. Maximum period for stay in the Guest House is 03 (Three) Days except for newly appointed staff of SAU.
  8. Accommodation in the Guest House will be allotted in the following order of preference:
    1. Teaching/academic/administrative staff visiting from outside Delhi for official work of the South Asian University (SAU).
    2. New appointees who come to join duty in the SAU.
    3. Members of staff of the other Universities/statutory educational institutions visiting SAU on university’s work/personal academic work.
    4. Academician visiting from other National and International Universities/Institutions.
    5. Family members of the employees of the University.
    6. Parents/Siblings of SAU students.

3. Cafeteria TimingMeal hours and ordering

To avail the breakfast, lunch and dinner, guests are requested to place the advance order preferably at the time of checking in. Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner shall be served at the cafeteria only, strictly during the following hours:

Breakfast7:30 a.m. – 9:30 a.m.
Lunch1:00 p.m. – 2:00 p.m.
Dinner7:30 p.m. – 9:30 p.m.
  • All meals, except Bed Tea and Tea, will be served only in the Dining Hall. (Room rent does not include any meals except bed tea)
Note: Guests may order food from outside; however, they are kindly requested to consume the same only in the designated dining area located on the ground floor of the Guest House. Consumption of food inside the guest rooms is discouraged.

4. General RulesConduct, safety and room use

✅ You must

  • Sign the Guest Register at check-in and check-out
  • Give a copy of your valid ID proof
  • Return your room key to reception before leaving
  • Foreign nationals: fill a ‘C’ form on arrival

🚫 Not allowed

  • Liquor, tobacco, pan or any drugs
  • Smoking (no smoking zone)
  • Pets
  • Cooking inside rooms
  • Additional persons in rooms

Full text

  1. Guests are advised to follow the rules mentioned below:
    1. All guests must sign the Guest Register kept on the front desk counter before check-in and check-out. All the guests have to compulsorily provide a copy of their valid ID proof for record of the guest house.
    2. An inventory of articles is available in the rooms. The responsibility for missing items or for breakage will rest with the guest(s) and will be chargeable.
    3. Only the guests are allowed inside the rooms. Guests will not be allowed to keep any additional person in their rooms. Other than the guests, no unauthorized persons who are accompanying the guests are allowed to enter the guest rooms.
    4. Guests will be provided with 2 towels (bath towel and face towel), toiletries and bed linen, 2 No’s. of 1 litre water bottles (per day) and sachets of tea, coffee and milk during their stay.
    5. Guests are not allowed to use any kind of liquor, tobacco, pan etc. and any types of drugs inside the guest house.
    6. The University guest house is a no smoking zone. Hence, smoking is strictly prohibited.
    7. If the guest is accompanying kids, the responsibility of looking after them lies with the guest only.
    8. Pets are not allowed in the guest house.
    9. The laundry facilities will be provided to guests under chargeable basis if required.
    10. Cooking inside the rooms is not permitted.
    11. Guests must deposit keys of their rooms at the reception before leaving the Guest House.
  2. All foreign nationals on arrival must fill a ‘C’ form and get their passport checked in the office.
💳

5. Payment & CancellationPaying bills and cancelling a booking
FreeCancel more than 48 hours before the date of stay: no cancellation charge.
20%Cancel within 48 hours of the date of stay: 20% of the total booking cost is charged.
  1. Bills are to be cleared during office hours i.e. 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. on working days. In case a guest needs to leave on a Saturday/Sunday or holiday, the payments should be made a day earlier.
  2. For cancellation of confirmed bookings, no cancellation charge is levied if booking is cancelled before 48 hours from date of stay. A cancellation charge of 20% of total booking cost shall be levied for cancellation within 48 hours from date of stay.
Not withstanding any provision in the aforesaid rules, the management of the South Asian University Guest House reserves the right to refuse booking or cancel the accommodation in the Guest House at any stage without assigning any reason.

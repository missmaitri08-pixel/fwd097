Guest House Rules & Regulations

To ensure a pleasant, secure, and disciplined stay for all guests, please adhere to the following university campus guidelines.

1. Check-In & Check-Out Timings

  • Standard Check-In Time: 12:00 PM (Noon).
  • Standard Check-Out Time: 11:00 AM.
  • Official photo identification (Passport, Aadhaar Card, or Government ID) is mandatory for all guests upon arrival.
  • Requests for early check-in or late check-out must be submitted in advance and are subject to room availability and desk approval.

2. Eligibility & Reservation Policy

  • Priority allocation is granted to official university delegates, visiting faculty, conference speakers, external examiners, and student families.
  • Subletting or transferring assigned rooms to non-registered individuals is strictly prohibited.
  • All booking requests are subject to verification and confirmation by the South Asian University administration.

3. Code of Conduct & Campus Discipline

  • The SAU Campus is a designated tobacco-free and alcohol-free zone. Smoking, alcohol consumption, or prohibited substances are strictly banned.
  • Quiet hours are observed between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM to respect the academic environment.
  • Guests are held responsible for maintaining room cleanliness and avoiding damage to university property.
  • External visitors are permitted only in the ground-floor reception lounge between 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM.

4. Payment & Cancellation Guidelines

  • Room tariffs must be settled prior to check-in or at check-out as indicated in your booking confirmation notice.
  • Cancellations submitted at least 48 hours prior to check-in incur no cancellation penalty.

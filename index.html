Booking Guidelines & FAQs

Important information regarding check-in policies and guest eligibility.

Who is eligible to book accommodation at the SAU Guest House?

Official university guests, keynote speakers, conference delegates, external academic examiners, and parents/guardians of enrolled students are eligible, subject to prior approval and room availability.

What are the standard check-in and check-out timings?

Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM (Noon) and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-ins or late extensions must be coordinated directly with the Guest House desk.

How is the payment processed for confirmed bookings?

Once your booking enquiry is reviewed and approved by the administration, an online payment link or bank transfer detail will be shared via email to finalize your reservation.

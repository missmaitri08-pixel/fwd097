Guesthouse at South Asian University

Academic hospitality in a tranquil, functional campus setting.

Welcome to the Guesthouse at South Asian University (SAU). The Guesthouse is designed to offer comfortable and convenient accommodation for the university’s official guests, including visiting faculty, external examiners, dignitaries, and participants of academic events such as conferences, seminars, and workshops.

Strategically located and thoughtfully maintained, the guesthouse supports the university’s commitment to academic hospitality and cross-border collaboration. It provides a tranquil and functional environment that enables guests to engage in the university’s academic and cultural life with ease.

Booking Guidelines & FAQs

Important information regarding check-in policies and guest eligibility.

Who is eligible to book accommodation at the SAU Guest House?

Official university guests, visiting faculty, external examiners, dignitaries, participants of conferences, seminars, and workshops, and permitted guests are eligible, subject to approval and room availability.

What are the standard check-in and check-out timings?

Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM (Noon) and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-ins or late extensions must be coordinated directly with the Guest House desk.

How is the payment processed for confirmed bookings?

Once your booking enquiry is reviewed and approved by the administration, payment instructions or bank details are shared to finalize your reservation.

About Our Guest House

An institutional haven designed for academic excellence, international cooperation, and serene stays.

The South Asian University (SAU) Campus Guest House is established to cater to the lodging and hosting requirements of international delegates, academic scholars, keynote speakers, external examiners, university guests, and families visiting students on campus. Established by the member nations of SAARC, SAU provides an environment where regional learning meets international hospitality standards.

Located within the peaceful university campus in Maidan Garhi, New Delhi, our Guest House offers standard rooms, executive VIP suites, and well-equipped meeting and event halls. Managed under strict institutional guidelines, we prioritize safety, high hygiene standards, and round-the-clock support to ensure a seamless stay for every guest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information for official guests, visiting scholars, and student guardians.

Who is eligible to stay at the SAU Guest House?

Accommodation is open to university delegates, guest faculty, external examiners, seminar speakers, permitted private guests, and parents or guardians of enrolled students, subject to departmental approval.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM (Noon) and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Any request for early check-in or late checkout must be coordinated in advance with the guest house front desk.

How can I submit a room booking request?

You can click on the "Book Now" link in the top menu to fill out the digital accommodation requisition form, or contact the administrative desk directly via email.

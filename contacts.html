How to Reach Us

Choose how you are travelling to see step-by-step directions.

Yellow LineChhatarpur or Qutub Minar station
Radiance Motelthe landmark to look for
Gaushala Roadleads to the SAU main gate

By Metro

Distance: ~4.5 km

  1. Get off at Chhatarpur Metro Station (Yellow Line) or Qutub Minar Station.
  2. Take an auto-rickshaw, battery rickshaw, or Grameen Seva vehicle heading toward Satbari and get dropped off at Radiance Motel.
  3. From there, the SAU campus is approximately 1 km walking distance.

By Car

Distance: ~4.5 km

  1. From Chhatarpur Metro Station, follow the road toward Chhatarpur Mandir and continue toward Satbari until you see Radiance Motel on your left (3.4 km).
  2. Take an immediate left onto Gaushala Road for 1 km.
  3. At the T-point, turn slight left to arrive at the SAU main gate.

⚠️ Important Route Note

GPS map navigation may suggest making a left turn prior to reaching Gaushala Road. Please ignore those early turns and continue straight toward Satbari until you reach the entrance of Gaushala Road near Radiance Motel.
